How the points are worked out

Points = 800 × (height ÷ species average) ÷ max XXL ratio + 150 × (weight ÷ species average) ÷ (max XXL ratio + 0.5) + 50 × (IV total ÷ 45), plus 178 for an XXL Pokémon. This is the formula players worked out; the game doesn't publish it.

Pokémon GO rounds height and weight on screen, so the app shows the possible range. Type and Great Buddy showcases score each Pokémon against its own species, so the same number applies there. Pumpkaboo and Gourgeist are different: their size tag tells you the form (XS is Small, no tag is Average, XL is Large, XXL is Super), and only Super Size gets the XXL bonus.

Species sizes come from PokémonDB's Pokémon GO data, with newer species filled in from the game's data. IV bars are measured from the screenshot itself.
